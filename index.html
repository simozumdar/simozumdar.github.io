About Me

Academic & Research Profile

I am an academic and researcher with over 12 years of teaching experience at the college level in Bangladesh. Throughout my career, I have been dedicated to nurturing young minds and fostering a passion for the life sciences among my students.

Currently, I am pursuing my Ph.D in Environmental Biotechnology at the Faculty of Biotechnology and Biomolecular Sciences, Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM) — one of Asia's leading research universities. My doctoral research focuses on the intersection of biotechnology and environmental sustainability.

Alongside my academic journey, I have served for over 10 years as Chairman of the Science and Environment Development Society (SEDS), Bangladesh — a non-governmental organisation committed to environmental conservation, scientific awareness, and sustainable community development across Bangladesh.
